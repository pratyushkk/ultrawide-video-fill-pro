Privacy Policy for UltraWide Video Fill Pro

Effective Date: September 27, 2026
Last Updated: September 27, 2026

UltraWide Video Fill Pro ("the Extension", "we", "our") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This policy explains our zero-data collection standards and local-only processing practices.

1. Zero Data Collection & Storage

  • No Personal Information: UltraWide Video Fill Pro does NOT collect, harvest, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, browsing history, user credentials, IP addresses, or device IDs.
  • No Analytics or Telemetry: There are NO third-party trackers, advertising SDKs, or background analytics embedded within the extension.

2. Local Device Processing Only

  • Client-Side Processing: All video aspect ratio adjustments, CSS scaling, GPU-accelerated sharpening, HDR boost filters, and brightness adjustments run 100% locally on your browser and GPU.
  • No Video Interception: Video streams and playback frames never leave your computer and are never uploaded to any remote server.

3. Local Storage Usage

The Extension uses standard browser chrome.storage.local solely to remember your preferred settings locally on your computer:

  • Aspect ratio presets (21:9, 32:9, 16:9, etc.)
  • Custom zoom/crop values
  • Global brightness preference
  • GPU enhancement toggles (Sharpness & HDR Boost)

These preferences never leave your local device.

4. Permissions Justification

  • <all_urls>: Required to detect and scale HTML5 video elements across any website you visit. No web content or user data is ever read or captured.
  • storage: Required solely to save your local video preferences on your device.
  • activeTab & scripting: Used to apply video styling and aspect ratio transformations to the active tab upon user interaction.

5. DRM Protected Content

On DRM-protected sites (e.g., Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, Disney+ Hotstar), the Extension operates in a hardware-safe mode that applies non-intrusive CSS transformations and bypasses canvas captures to guarantee uninterrupted playback.
